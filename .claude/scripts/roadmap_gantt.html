How the harness runs tickets in parallel

TKT-16

One worktree per ticket

ticket_tree.py prepare gives each ticket its own worktree, <repository>-tickets/<ID>/ on feature/<ID>, from origin/develop. The main checkout stays on develop, so several /ticket-run work side by side. Still one commit per ticket.

Start rule

Start when the prerequisites are merged

At the gate of /ticket-run, tickets.py check refuses a ticket whose prerequisites are not merged on origin/develop, or a field ticket before its campaign. /ticket-run <ID> --force starts anyway, and logs why.

Conflicts

Lock areas

The same check refuses a ticket while an open ticket changes one of the same services, front ends or packages. The charts apply this rule: without it, even 6 lanes only finish in __K6_NOLOCK__ days instead of __K4_DAYS__ with 4, and you pay that back in merge conflicts.

TKT-15

A pull request per ticket

ticket_tree.py finish rebases the ticket's commit on origin/develop when it moved, pushes feature/<ID> and opens its pull request. The automerge workflow merges it once every check passed, and its issue closes.

Bottleneck

Your review

With 4 tickets at once you are busy __K4_BUSY__ % of the time approving plans and reviewing, about __K4_HOURS__ hours a day. More lanes add nothing. Phase 0 already plans line-by-line review only for identity and isolation, with the rest reviewed on tests and screens. Applying that rule halves the review time, which brings 4 lanes to __K4L_DAYS__ days.

Board, VS Code, budget

How this was computed

  • Prerequisites were read from each ticket row of the eight phase pages of docs/specs/roadmap/: what a ticket needs from another before it can be built and tested. There are __LINKS__ links, judged from the text and kept in docs/specs/roadmap/gantt/ticket-graph.json. They are an estimate, to correct when a ticket is planned. python3 .claude/scripts/roadmap_gantt.py build rebuilds this page and the roadmap page from it, with the tickets merged so far.
  • Durations come from the PROGRESS.md timings of the merged tickets. The merged tickets took __AGENT_MIN__ minutes of agent work on average. The model gives a medium ticket __M_MIN__ minutes, to allow for later tickets being larger: small 0.7 h, medium 1.3 h and large 2.6 h in all, agent work first, then your time (approval and review).
  • Simulation: K lanes. A ticket holds its lane and its areas from its start until it is merged, and one person reviews tickets one after another. On the critical path, the ticket with the most work after it goes first.
  • External dates (G, H, P and C items) are the roadmap's, unchanged. Some can be brought forward because they are your own actions, such as registration (G-01), certificates (G-02, G-03) and purchases (H-xx). Others cannot: the authority's decision time for G-05, the farming seasons and the event dates.
  • Not included: holidays, fix rounds beyond those already measured, and rework found in the field.
