## <a id="statement"></a>📌 Statement

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every screen, message, [🔤 Alert](../../glossary.md#term-alert) and report of Swarm MUST be available in English, French, German and Spanish, and each person MUST be able to choose their language.

## <a id="rationale"></a>💡 Rationale

Operators and Clients work across Europe and the US. A Pilot must read Alerts in the language they think in.

## <a id="examples"></a>🧪 Examples

| # | Case | Given | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ✅ Allowed | A Pilot chooses French | Ground control, Alerts and the Pre-flight check are in French |
| 2 | ❌ Refused | A new screen is delivered in English only | Not accepted |

## <a id="exceptions"></a>⚠️ Exceptions

> [!WARNING]
> Names from the Glossary of a Client (Sites, Drones) are not translated.
