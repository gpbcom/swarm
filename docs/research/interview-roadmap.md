# 🛣️ Roadmap interview

**Date:** 2026-11-18. **Interviewee:** the product owner. These answers are the source of the roadmap in
[docs/specs/roadmap/](../specs/roadmap/README.md).

| # | Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Start and horizon | 4 January 2027, 24 months (52 sprints of two weeks) |
| R2 | Team | One person who steers and codes, with Claude Code agents splitting each ticket into parallel tasks |
| R3 | Scope | Every phase, P0 to P7, within the 24 months |
| R4 | Order | Inspection first, then Patrol, Agriculture, AI agents, Light shows, scale and reach |
| R5 | Pages | One page per phase plus cross-cutting tracks (regulation, flight tests, hardware and partners, risks, traceability) |
| R6 | Flight tests | The person flies as the Pilot with a small fleet, Open category then Specific category Authorisation |
| R7 | Launch | Design-partner Operator from month 6, betas per Activity, general availability in month 24 |
| R8 | Details | 13 cells, signal retention, SLOs, sizing and front-end budgets as confirmed |
| R9 | M1 | One real Drone at a time in the Open category through the whole Squad pipeline; the 4-Drone Squad in the Simulator; the first real Squad flight in C-02 after the Authorisation |
| R10 | Capacity | About 6 tickets per sprint accepted in S41 to S52 |
| R11 | Cells before general availability | An OVHcloud cell from S24 and a self-hosted beta cell; Terraform for infrastructure as code |
| R12 | Details | Seed dose in kg/ha; missing Agriculture licences fail the Pre-flight check; detection quality gate (recall 90 %, precision 80 %); DJI and Skydio Drones rented and a blockchain aggregator account in S47; betas supported from the running phases |
